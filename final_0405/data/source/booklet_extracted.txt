Source: greenshied booklet.pdf (26 pages)
Plan: University of Ottawa Graduate Students' Association
Billing Divisions: 23785 and 32012
Effective Date: September 1, 2025
Benefit Year: September 1 - August 31

============================================================
HEALTH BENEFIT PLAN
============================================================

Overall Deductible: Nil
Overall Maximum:
  - Prescription Drugs: $2,000 per person per benefit year
  - All Other Health Benefits: $5,000 per person per benefit year (excluding Gender Affirmation)

------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRESCRIPTION DRUGS
------------------------------------------------------------
Method: Pay Direct Drug Card
Requirements: prescribed by physician/dentist, legally requires prescription, has DIN
Co-pays and Maximums:
  - All covered drugs: 20% co-pay, $2,000/yr max
  - Contraceptives (oral): 10% co-pay, $350/yr (within drug max)
  - Contraceptives (injections/IUD/patch): 20% co-pay, $350/yr (within drug max)
  - HPV vaccines: 20% co-pay, reasonable & customary (within drug max)
  - All other vaccines: 50% co-pay
Notes:
  - Generic substitution enforced unless physician specifies no substitution
  - Maintenance drugs may require 90-day supply
  - Prior authorization may be required for some drugs
  - Includes insulin and diabetic supplies (syringes, needles, testing agents) if approved
NOT covered:
  - Obesity, erectile dysfunction, infertility drugs
  - Non-prescription vitamins
  - Smoking cessation products
  - Medical cannabis
  - Experimental/unapproved drugs
  - Compounds not conforming to GreenShield compound policy

------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOSPITAL
------------------------------------------------------------
Coverage: Semi-private room in public hospital
Co-pay: 50%
Maximum: 5 days per disability
Includes: public general, convalescent, rehabilitation, chronic hospital

------------------------------------------------------------
3. MEDICAL ITEMS AND SERVICES
------------------------------------------------------------

a) Aids for daily living: 0% co-pay, reasonable & customary
   - hospital beds (including rails, mattresses)
   - bedpans
   - standard commodes
   - decubitus (bedridden) supplies
   - I.V. stands
   - portable patient lifts
   - trapezes/transfer poles
   - urinals

b) Braces and casts: 50% co-pay, $500/yr combined

c) Incontinence/Ostomy equipment: 0% co-pay
   - catheter supplies
   - ostomy supplies

d) Mobility aids: 0% co-pay
   - canes
   - crutches
   - walkers
   - wheelchairs (including batteries)

e) Standard prosthetics: 0% co-pay
   - arm, hand, leg, foot, breast, eye, larynx

f) Respiratory/Cardiology equipment: 0% co-pay
   - compressors
   - inhalant devices
   - tracheotomy supplies
   - oxygen

g) Compression Stockings: 50% co-pay, $500/yr
   - Must have pressure measurement of 15 mmHg or higher

Notes:
  - Some items may require pre-authorization
  - Rental vs purchase decision based on physician estimate of duration
  - Rental price will not exceed purchase price

------------------------------------------------------------
4. GENDER AFFIRMATION
------------------------------------------------------------
Requires: diagnosis of gender dysphoria/incongruence from physician or nurse practitioner
Maximum: $10,000 lifetime
Co-pay: 0%
Foundation (core): genital/chest surgeries, vocal surgery, tracheal shave, chest contouring,
                   vaginal dilators, laser hair removal, facial feminization
Focused: nose surgery, liposuction/lipofilling, face/eyelid lift, lip/cheek fillers,
         hair transplant/implants, gluteal lifts/implants

------------------------------------------------------------
5. EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION
------------------------------------------------------------
Coverage: professional land or air ambulance to nearest equipped hospital
Co-pay: 0%
Maximum: $250 per benefit year
Condition: medically required due to injury, illness, or acute physical disability

------------------------------------------------------------
6. PRIVATE DUTY NURSING IN THE HOME
------------------------------------------------------------
Coverage: Registered Nurse (R.N.) services in home
Co-pay: 0%
Minimum: 8 hours per shift
Requires: Pre-Authorization Form from attending physician
NOT covered: custodial services or services not requiring R.N. skill level

------------------------------------------------------------
7. PROFESSIONAL SERVICES
------------------------------------------------------------
Requires: practitioner licensed by provincial regulatory agency or recognized professional association

Group A — $50/visit, $500/yr per practitioner:
  - Chiropractor
  - Registered Massage Therapist
  - Naturopath
  - Speech Therapist
  - Physiotherapist

Group B — $80/visit, $1,000/yr combined:
  - Psychologist
  - Psychotherapist
  - Social Worker
  - Clinical Counsellor
  - Master of Social Work

Group C — $80/visit, combined with Group B $1,000/yr max:
  - Holistic Nutritional Consultant

------------------------------------------------------------
8. ACCIDENTAL DENTAL
------------------------------------------------------------
Coverage: dental care for natural teeth damaged by direct blow to mouth
Co-pay: 0%
Maximum: reasonable and customary charges
Conditions:
  - NOT caused by object placed in mouth
  - Must notify GreenShield immediately after accident
  - Treatment must start within 180 days of accident
  - Services covered up to 365 days after accident or end of coverage
  - Limited to one set of artificial teeth
NOT covered: periodontia, orthodontia, repair/replacement of artificial teeth

------------------------------------------------------------
9. VISION
------------------------------------------------------------
a) Prescription eyeglasses or contact lenses: $150 per 24 months (from date of first paid claim)
b) Medically necessary contact lenses: included in above
   (for keratoconus, irregular astigmatism, etc.)
c) Optometric eye exams: $75 for one exam every 2 years
   (only in provinces where not covered by provincial plan)
d) Replacement parts for prescription eyeglasses: included
e) Non-prescription sunglasses: only if prescribed for specific ophthalmic disease

NOT covered: medical/surgical treatment, orthoptics, vision training, subnormal vision aids,
             contact lens fitting follow-ups, eyeglass cases

------------------------------------------------------------
10. TUTORIAL BENEFIT
------------------------------------------------------------
Coverage: private tutorial service of qualified teacher
Co-pay: 0%
Maximum: $15/hr, up to $1,000/yr
Condition: confined to home/hospital for minimum 30 consecutive days (physician recommendation required)
Note: Dependents NOT eligible

============================================================
DENTAL BENEFIT PLAN
============================================================

Deductible: Nil
Fee Guide: current less 1 year Provincial Dental Association Fee Guide for General Practitioners
Overall Maximum: $1,000 per person per benefit year for all dental services combined

------------------------------------------------------------
BASIC SERVICES (0% co-pay)
------------------------------------------------------------
Diagnostic & Preventive:
  - Complete oral exam: 1x per 3 years
  - Emergency/specific oral exams
  - Full series/panoramic X-rays: 1x per 3 years
  - Bitewing X-rays: 1x per 6 months
  - Recall exams: 1x per 6 months
  - Cleaning (1 unit polishing + 1 unit scaling): 1x per recall period
  - Denture cleaning: 1x per 6 months
  - Pit/fissure sealants on molars: age 14 and under only
  - Space maintainers
  - Protective mouth guards: 1x per 12 months
  - Periodontal scaling: 3 time-units per benefit year

Basic Restorative (25% co-pay):
  - Amalgam, tooth-coloured fillings, temporary sedative fillings
  - Inlay restorations (paid at non-bonded amalgam rate)

Basic Oral Surgery (0% co-pay):
  - Extractions (2 wisdom teeth per benefit year)
  - General anaesthesia/sedation with eligible oral surgery only

------------------------------------------------------------
COMPREHENSIVE BASIC SERVICES (25% co-pay)
------------------------------------------------------------
Standard Denture Services:
  - Denture repairs/tooth additions
  - Relining/rebasing: 1x per 3 years (after 6 months from installation)
  - Adjustments: after 3 months from installation
  - Soft tissue conditioning linings
  - Partial denture remake using existing framework: 1x per 5 years

Comprehensive Oral Surgery:
  - Surgical exposure, repositioning, transplantation
  - Bone/gum remodeling
  - Cyst/tumor removal
  - Drainage/exploration
  - Fracture treatment
  - Frenectomy

Endodontic Treatment (70% co-pay):
  - Root canal therapy
  - Pulpotomy/pulpectomy
  - Apexification
  - Apical curettage, root resections
  - Root amputation/hemisection
  - Non-vital tooth bleaching
  - Emergency opening/draining

Periodontal Treatment (included in basic):
  - 3 time-units per benefit year (15 min per unit)

------------------------------------------------------------
MAJOR SERVICES (70% co-pay)
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Standard onlays/crown restorations: 1x per 5 years
  - Crown/onlay/bridge repair or recementing

============================================================
EXCLUSIONS (both Health and Dental)
============================================================
  - War, riot, criminal activity
  - Armed forces service
  - Missed appointments
  - Non-emergency treatment outside Canada
  - Form translation charges
  - Medical cannabis
  - Experimental treatments
  - Off-label drug use
  - Cosmetic/aesthetic procedures
  - Services by family members
  - Lost/stolen item replacement (unless natural wear)
  - Insulin pumps (unless specifically included)
  - Dental implants
  - Sleep dentistry
  - TMJ treatment appliances

============================================================
CLAIM SUBMISSION
============================================================
ID Format: GSA + student ID number (e.g., GSA111222333-00)
Deadline: 12 months from date of expense
Methods: Pay-Direct Card, GreenShield+ app/portal, mail
Required: original itemized paid receipt (not just cash/credit card receipt)
Contact: Customer Service 1.888.525.7587 or greenshield.ca

============================================================
COORDINATION OF BENEFITS (COB)
============================================================
- This plan is ALWAYS primary for the plan member
- Spouse's plan is primary for spouse's own claims
- Children: parent with earlier birthday (month/day) in calendar year is primary
- Submit to primary first, then unpaid balance to secondary
- Can be reimbursed up to 100% of eligible expense across plans
