uOttawa Student Health Plan — Detailed Coverage (Undergraduate)
Source: https://studentcare.ca/plan/en-CA/IHaveAPlan_Ottawa/understanding-my-coverage
Retrieved: 2026-03-08

Note: This is the undergraduate plan from studentcare.ca. GSA (graduate) plan may differ slightly.
See uottawa_gsa_plan_coverage.txt for graduate-specific info.

=== PRESCRIPTION DRUGS & VACCINATIONS ===

- Coverage: up to 80%
- Annual maximum: $1,000
- Dispensing fee cap: $5 per prescription/refill
- Eligible contraceptives: combined max $300

=== MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES ===

- Psychologists, MSW, Social Workers, Counsellors, Psychotherapists
- Coverage: 80% per visit
- Annual maximum: $1,500 combined

=== ALLIED HEALTH PRACTITIONERS ===

All at 100% up to $40/visit, $500 annual combined maximum across all:
- Chiropractors
- Physiotherapists (MD or nurse practitioner referral every 12 months)
- Occupational therapists
- Homeopaths
- Podiatrists
- Osteopaths
- Speech therapists
- Naturopaths
- Massage therapists (MD referral needed)
- Dietitians
- Acupuncturists

=== VISION CARE ===

- Prescription glasses or contacts: $150 every 24 months
- Eye exams: included in above or separate (check booklet)
- Medically necessary contact lenses: $200 every 24 months

=== DENTAL ===

- Annual maximum: $750 per policy year
- Recall exams/cleanings: 70%
- Fillings/basic oral surgery: 75%
- Root canals/gum treatments/dentures: 75%
- Extractions: 50%
- X-rays/scaling: covered

=== TRAVEL ===

- Duration: 120 days per trip
- Medical incident: $5,000,000 lifetime maximum
- Trip cancellation: $3,000 per trip
- Trip interruption: $7,500 per trip

=== ADDITIONAL SERVICES ===

- Virtual healthcare (HI - Dialogue) for full-time/part-time undergraduates
- Mental health/wellness program (EmpowerMe)

=== CLAIM SUBMISSION ===

Group Number: UNV
Member ID: UNV + student ID (up to 10 digits) + 2-digit dependent code

Four submission methods:
1. Pay-Direct Card — present at provider
2. Mobile App — GreenShield+ app
3. Online Portal — app.greenshieldplus.ca
4. Mail — download claim form, attach docs, mail

All claims must be received within 12 months of expense date.
