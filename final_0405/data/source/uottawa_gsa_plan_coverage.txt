uOttawa GSA Student Health Plan — Coverage Summary
Source: https://www.greenshield.ca/en-ca/student-centre/uottawa-grads-gsa
Retrieved: 2026-03-08

Insurer: GreenShield (Group Number: UNV)
Policy Year: Sept. 1 - Aug. 31 (or Jan. 1 - Aug. 31 for new January students)

=== HEALTH COVERAGE ===

Prescription Drugs & Vaccinations
- Coverage: 80%
- Annual maximum: $1,000
- Dispensing fee capped at $5 per prescription/refill
- Eligible contraceptives: combined max $300
  - Oral contraceptives: 10% co-pay
  - Injections, IUDs, patches: 20% co-pay ($350 max within drug maximum)
- All other vaccines: 50% co-pay

Health Benefits (general)
- $5,000 per covered person per benefit, excluding Gender Affirmation

Hospital
- Semi-private room in public hospitals: 50% co-pay
- Limited to 5 days per disability

Professional Services
- Chiropractors, RMTs, naturopaths, speech/physio therapists: $50/visit, $500 annual limit per practitioner
- Mental health providers (psychologists, psychotherapists, counselors): $80/visit, $1,000 combined annual limit
- Holistic nutritional consultants: included in mental health provider limits

Braces & Casts
- 50% co-pay, $500 per benefit year combined

Compression Stockings
- 50% co-pay, $500 per benefit year

Emergency Transportation
- $250 per benefit year

=== DENTAL COVERAGE ===

- Combined maximum: $1,000 per benefit year
- Basic preventive/diagnostic: no co-pay
- Basic restorative: 25% co-pay
- Major services: 70% co-pay

=== VISION COVERAGE ===

- Prescription glasses or contacts: $150 every 2 years
- Eye exams: $75 every 2 years

=== ACCIDENTAL DENTAL ===

- No co-pay, reasonable and customary charges covered
